# MeraVyapar AI — LinkedIn Announcement Draft

Excited to unveil **MeraVyapar AI** — my submission for the **Great Indian AI Internship Challenge (Real Deal Award)**! 🇮🇳🚀

Most AI finance apps are thin wrappers around cloud LLMs that hallucinate balances and upload private bank statements. I wanted to build something genuinely useful for Indian Kirana stores, traders, and small business merchants.

**What makes MeraVyapar AI different?**
🔒 **Local-First & Private**: Embedded SQLite ledger with exact integer paise math. Zero financial data leaves the device.
🎙️ **Multilingual Voice Autopilot**: Powered by **Gnani AI** (Prisma v2.5 STT & Timbre v2.5 TTS). Merchants can speak in Hindi or English, and activate hands-free with the custom foreground wake phrase **"Namaste Vyapar"**.
📊 **Financial Intelligence & Forecasting**: On-device 14-day cash flow projections and counterparty concentration metrics with strict sparse-history protections.
✨ **PhonePe-Inspired UI**: Original white-and-purple design language with seamless dark mode and instant responsiveness.
🛡️ **Zero Autonomous Risk**: Built-in confirmation safeguards—voice commands never silently mutate financial records without explicit review.

Huge thanks to the challenge organizers and Gnani AI for powering vernacular conversational AI for Bharat!
