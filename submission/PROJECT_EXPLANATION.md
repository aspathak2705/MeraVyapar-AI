# MeraVyapar AI — Project Explanation
## Great Indian AI Internship Challenge — Real Deal Award Submission

### 1. Vision & Mission
MeraVyapar AI is an Indian merchant financial intelligence application tailored for retailers, Kirana store operators, traders, and small business owners. Unlike generic expense trackers or cloud accounting tools, MeraVyapar AI operates on a **local-first, privacy-by-design architecture** with **multilingual voice autopilot powered by Gnani AI**.

### 2. Core Pillars
1. **Know the Money**: Exact integer paise calculations, deterministic net cash movement, receivables tracking, and payables management running 100% on-device in embedded SQLite.
2. **Understand the Business**: On-device Financial Intelligence Graph (FIG) and 14-day cash flow forecasting with strict sparse-history protections.
3. **Explain What Is Happening**: Factual LLM reasoning via OpenRouter Nemotron, strictly grounded in local financial snapshots with zero hallucinations.
4. **Hands-Free Conversational Autopilot**: Vernacular speech recognition (Gnani Prisma v2.5 STT) and natural voice synthesis (Gnani Timbre v2.5 TTS) activated by the custom foreground wake phrase **"Namaste Vyapar"**.
5. **Merchant-Controlled Action Center**: Verified payment reminder drafting and OS handoff with mandatory merchant review. Zero autonomous mutations or outbound messaging gateways.
