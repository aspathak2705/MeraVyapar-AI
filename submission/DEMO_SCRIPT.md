# MeraVyapar AI — Demo Video Script
## 3-Minute Walkthrough for Evaluators

### Scene 1: The Problem & Vision (0:00 - 0:30)
- **Visual**: Opening with PhonePe-style purple splash and login screen.
- **Narrative**: "Indian merchants manage millions of daily transactions, but cloud accounting tools leak private banking data and generic chatbots hallucinate balances. Meet MeraVyapar AI: a local-first, privacy-by-design financial autopilot with multilingual voice AI powered by Gnani AI."

### Scene 2: Local Security & Dashboard (0:30 - 1:00)
- **Visual**: Merchant unlocks app with 4-digit PIN. Dashboard opens showing Net Cash Movement hero card, Inflows, Outflows, and Receivables in ₹.
- **Narrative**: "Everything runs on-device in embedded SQLite using exact integer paise arithmetic. We never label cash movement as 'bank balance' and zero data leaks to the cloud."

### Scene 3: Hands-Free Voice Autopilot (1:00 - 2:00)
- **Visual**: Merchant speaks: *"Namaste Vyapar, Sharma ji ko payment reminder draft karo"*.
- **Narrative**: "Notice the foreground wake-word detection for 'Namaste Vyapar'. Gnani Prisma transcribes the Hindi speech, parses the payment promise, and presents a structured confirmation card. Spoken words never silently mutate the database—the merchant explicitly confirms, saving it to local SQLite with spoken feedback from Gnani Timbre."

### Scene 4: Financial Intelligence & Forecasting (2:00 - 2:40)
- **Visual**: Tapping the purple hero card to open the 14-day cash flow outlook and counterparty dependency analysis.
- **Narrative**: "Our on-device forecasting engine models daily cash movements. If transaction history is under 7 days, it shows an explicit Data Sufficiency Warning rather than guessing numbers."

### Scene 5: Offline Resilience & Closing (2:40 - 3:00)
- **Visual**: Turning on Airplane mode and toggling Dark Mode smoothly.
- **Narrative**: "Even without internet, the entire ledger, receivables tracker, and action center remain 100% operational. MeraVyapar AI: Real AI, real fintech engineering, built for Bharat."
